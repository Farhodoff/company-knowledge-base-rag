# Company Employee Onboarding Guide

## 1. Welcome to the Team
Welcome! This guide outlines the essential steps and expectations during your first 30 days at the company.

## 2. First Week Checklist
- **Day 1**:
  - Attend the HR welcome orientation at 10:00 AM.
  - Complete workstation setup, corporate email login, and 1Password vault invite.
  - Join primary Slack channels: `#announcements`, `#general`, `#engineering`, and `#team-random`.
- **Day 2**:
  - Meet your dedicated Onboarding Buddy for a 1-on-1 walkthrough.
  - Review the company Engineering Standards and Vacation Policy.
  - Clone team repositories and set up local Docker environments.
- **Day 3**:
  - Access JIRA / Project board and review the sprint goals.
  - Push your first "good first issue" Pull Request to test the CI/CD pipeline.
- **Day 4-5**:
  - Attend your first team daily standup (10:30 AM).
  - Schedule introductory 15-minute coffee chats with cross-functional team members.

## 3. Communication Channels & Working Hours
- **Core Hours**: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM local time (flexibility outside core hours).
- **Synchronous vs Asynchronous**:
  - Slack is for real-time discussions, incident coordination, and team questions.
  - Notion / GitHub Discussions are for RFCs, design docs, and architectural decisions.
  - Meeting policy: No-Meeting Wednesdays across all engineering squads.

## 4. Hardware & Equipment Stipend
- All full-time employees receive an Apple MacBook Pro (M-series) or certified Linux laptop.
- A home-office equipment stipend of $500 is provided upon completion of probation.
- Submit expense receipts via the BambooHR / Expensify portal under "Home Office Setup".

## 5. Probation Period & 30-60-90 Day Reviews
- Probation period duration is 3 months.
- Reviews are conducted at Day 30 (culture & onboarding check), Day 60 (initial contributions), and Day 90 (formal confirmation).
