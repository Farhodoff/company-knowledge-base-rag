# Vacation Policy

## Requesting Vacation

1. Submit a vacation request through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance.
2. Your manager must approve the request within 5 business days.
3. Once approved, add the dates to the team calendar.
4. Maximum consecutive vacation: 2 weeks (10 business days).

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

- 0-2 years: 15 days per year
- 2-5 years: 20 days per year
- 5+ years: 25 days per year

## How to Request

1. Log in to hr.company.com
2. Go to "Time Off" → "Request Vacation"
3. Select dates and add a note
4. Submit for manager approval
